## 🚀 Featured Project

### 🌱 Agralyticx

**Agralyticx** is an agricultural technology ecosystem designed to connect farmers, students/researchers, investors, transport agents, landowners, and agricultural companies through one platform.

The project focuses on:

* 🌾 Agricultural information & crop guidance
* 🤖 AI-assisted agricultural support
* 🗣️ Voice-first interaction
* 🌦️ Weather & disaster information
* 📈 Market / Mandi information
* 💰 Financial & budgeting assistance
* 🚚 Agricultural transportation
* 👥 Community & collaboration
* 🌍 Multilingual accessibility
* 🎓 Student & researcher opportunities
